Overview

HiFi Placement is an offline tool for loudspeaker placement and room acoustics simulation. It works entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information — we do not ask for your name, email, or identity.
  • No analytics or tracking — the app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.
  • No network access — the app makes no network requests of its own. It contains no ad networks, no third-party frameworks that communicate over the network.
  • No microphone or camera access — all acoustic results are computed from a physics model, not from measurements.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores your projects (room dimensions, placements, materials) and preferences (language, appearance) locally on your device only. This data:

  • never leaves your device,
  • is not backed up to any server by us (iCloud device backups, if you enable them, are managed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy),
  • is removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is processed entirely by Apple's App Store (StoreKit). We never see your payment details, Apple ID, or purchase receipts. Purchase entitlements are managed by Apple. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchase data.

Children's Privacy

The app is rated 4+, contains no content collection, and is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes (e.g. a future version adds optional features), the updated policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at the app's support repository (GitHub: Hawkingfan/hifiplacement-support).

简体中文

概述

HiFi Placement（HiFi 摆位）是一款完全离线运行的音箱摆位与房间声学模拟工具。所有计算均在你自己的设备上完成。我们不收集、不传输、不共享任何个人数据。

我们不收集的数据

  • 不收集个人信息——不要求姓名、邮箱或任何身份信息。
  • 无分析、无追踪——App 不含任何统计 SDK、广告与追踪行为。
  • 无联网行为——App 自身不发起任何网络请求，不含广告网络或任何联网第三方框架。
  • 不使用麦克风与相机——全部声学结果由物理模型计算得出，不做实测。

存储在你设备上的数据

App 将你的方案（房间尺寸、摆位、材质）与偏好设置（语言、外观）仅存储在本地设备。这些数据：

  • 永远不会离开你的设备；
  • 不会被我们备份到任何服务器（如你开启了 iCloud 设备备份，该备份由 Apple 依 Apple 隐私政策 管理）；
  • 删除 App 时即随之删除。

购买

可选的 Pro 升级由 Apple App Store（StoreKit）全流程处理。我们无法接触你的支付信息、Apple ID 或购买凭证，购买资格由 Apple 管理。Apple 如何处理购买数据见 Apple 隐私政策。

儿童隐私

App 分级 4+，无任何内容收集，全年龄适用。
