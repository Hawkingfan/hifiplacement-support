HiFi Placement — Support

Speaker placement & room acoustics optimizer for iPhone / iPad · Privacy Policy · 技术支持

Frequently Questions 常见问题

1. Does the app need a microphone or any measurements?

No. HiFi Placement is fully physics-based. Room response, modal analysis and sound field are all computed from a room model on your device — no microphone, no measuring, no network.

不需要。App 完全基于物理模型，房间频响、模态分析与声场分布全部在设备本地由声学引擎计算得出——无需麦克风、无需测量、无需联网。

2. What does the free version include? What is Pro?

Free: 3D placement studio, frequency response prediction, automatic project restore. Pro (subscription or one-time lifetime purchase): Sound Field CT heatmap, Placement Optimizer, Projects library and A/B Compare.

免费版：3D 摆位工作台、频响预测、方案自动恢复。Pro（订阅或一次性买断）：声场 CT 热图、摆位优化器、方案库与 A/B 对比。

3. How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Settings → Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account. Lifetime and subscription both restore this way.

设置 → 恢复购买，使用同一 Apple 账户登录即可。买断与订阅均可恢复。

4. The app is offline — how does it work without internet?

All computation runs locally with the built-in HiFiEngine v3 acoustic engine. That is why nothing you enter ever leaves your device.

全部计算由内置 HiFiEngine v3 声学引擎在本地完成，因此你的任何数据都不会离开设备。

5. Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS/iPadOS 18 or later, landscape optimized. Apple Vision Pro runs the iPad version.

支持 iOS/iPadOS 18 及以上的 iPhone 与 iPad，横屏优化；Apple Vision Pro 可运行 iPad 版。

6. How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Open an issue in this repository (GitHub: Hawkingfan/hifiplacement-support), or leave a review describing what happened — both reach the developer.

在本仓库（GitHub: Hawkingfan/hifiplacement-support）提交 issue，或在 App Store 评价中描述问题——开发者都会看到。
